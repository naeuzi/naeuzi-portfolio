02 About

Meet Alva.

Business owner · Account manager · Video editor · Marketing

I'm Alva — a 19-year-old video editor from Sweden, known online as NAEUZI.

My interest in editing started in 2015. In 2019 I moved over to Adobe After Effects and Premiere Pro and spent the next few years finding my niche — experimenting with anime edits, pop edits and more than a few different usernames along the way.

In 2023 NAEUZI was born. The account grew fast on Instagram, and in 2024 I brought it to TikTok, where it has since reached 1.2M followers and more than a billion views across platforms.

In 2025, at 18, I founded NAEUZI Studios AB — a Swedish company focused on digital marketing, account management and editing services.

What started as being a fan has turned into working on releases from the BLACKPINK members themselves.
